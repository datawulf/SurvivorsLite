Guide

Install

Download SurvivorsLite-<version>.zip from the latest release. The zip holds one folder named SurvivorsLite, which is what the game's installer wants. GitHub's "Download ZIP" of the source code does not install.

Browser game

  1. Open Options, AddOns, Install and pick the zip.
  2. Remove SurvivorsDragonUi on the same page so the two do not both run.
  3. The addon loads on your next run. A login line in chat says how to open the options.

A new version installs over the old one and keeps your settings.

Windows build

Unzip so the game's AddOns folder holds SurvivorsLite\ with SurvivorsLite.toc inside.

Options

Any of these opens the options window:

  • /slite or /sl
  • the gear on the minimap's edge
  • the gear at the end of the floating action bar
  • the SurvivorsLite button in the game menu (Esc)

Every setting changes live and has a tooltip. Esc closes the window. "Move frames" lets you drag the hero bar, action bar, party frames, status list and the circles' center. Reset asks for a second click.

Plates and the status list

  • Final boss: magenta skull. Bosses: orange skull and "ENRAGED" when they enrage. Guardians: their lair's mark, dimmed while asleep.
  • Bars show health in percent and as a number and your debuffs with stacks and time left.
  • Where several units share a name (an elite pack mob named like a guardian) a plate is matched by health, so only the real one is marked. Elites can be marked too (off by default).
  • Plain enemies: a thin health bar (default), no plate (/slite mobs hide) or the game's plate (/slite mobs show).

The status list has one short line per boss and guardian. Lines whose unit has its plate up near you are bright.

Lairs and marks

Every guardian's lair has a mark of its own (star, circle, diamond, triangle, moon, square) by its place in the run's lair line. The same mark is on its minimap pin, its status line and its guardian's plate, so "the triangle one" is easy to find.

  • Minimap pins: grey while the lair refills and a red "!" while its guardian is awake. A pin off the minimap sits on the edge and points the way.
  • The lair that comes back first is bigger, glows gold and counts down under its pin. Its tooltip says when and how far, for example "Back in 0:45, 86 yd away. Comes back first."
  • Chests are pinned as well. Bosses are not: the game sends no position for them.

Reset range (leash)

The leash is how far from its lair you can lead a guardian before it gives up and goes home. The game does not tell addons, so it starts at the setting (40 yd) with a "?" after it and is learned in play, both ways:

  • While a guardian keeps chasing you, the farthest you stood from its lair counts as a distance it holds at, once it is still awake two seconds later. The leash grows to it.
  • When an awake guardian falls back asleep alive while you live, where you stood is where it gave up. The addon says so in chat and the "?" goes away.

While a guardian chases you a bar over your character shows it, for example "Mother Fang 32 / 40 yd", green, orange then red, and "gives up soon" at about 85 percent. The minimap draws a ring at the leash around each held lair.

The distance is measured from you to the lair through your map position, so it needs the run's lair line and a zone map. The Guardian leash slider or /slite leash 45 sets it by hand and forgets what play taught.

Not confirmed in game yet. If the "?" never goes away after a guardian resets on you, set the leash by hand and please report it.

Circles

  • Range circle: blue, 8 yd by default (/slite range 8). An optional orange second circle (/slite range2 30). Both can grow with your Area stat.
  • Pickup circle: gold, at your pickup radius from the run's stats.
  • Skill circles: in the options under "Skill circles" every skill on your bar with a reach is listed with its color and yards. Tick Eviscerate and Blade Flurry and each gets its own circle. The reach comes from the skill's tooltip ("8 yd radius", "12 yd range", "within 12 yd") exactly as the game writes it, so it grows with ranks, upgrades and, for the skills it applies to, Area. Picks are remembered by skill name.

The circles are drawn above the world, so trees do not cover them. While the options are open or frames are unlocked each circle shows its radius.

Circles on the ground

The client has no addon call for the camera, but 1.12's saved views carry it: SaveView(5) writes the camera's distance and pitch into the cameraDistanceD and cameraPitchD settings. The addon reads them twice a second (ten times a second right after the camera moved) while a circle shows and projects every circle onto flat ground around your feet. View 5's own values are put back at logout.

  • The options window shows what it reads under Camera, and /slite camera says it in chat.
  • "Waiting for the camera" after you zoomed or turned the camera means this client does not write live values into saved views. The circles then stay flat.
  • Match Field of view once: turn on Pickup radius and change Field of view until experience starts flying to you as it touches the gold circle.
  • The ground is taken as flat at your feet, so circles drift on slopes and lag the camera's smoothing by up to half a second.

Not confirmed in game yet.

Flat circles

Without the live camera the circles are flat on the screen and need one calibration for the zoom you play at:

  1. Zoom out the way you play and open /slite.
  2. Turn on Pickup radius (it shows once a run sends your stats).
  3. Change Size per yard until experience starts flying to you as it touches the gold circle. The blue circle is then right too.
  4. If your camera looks in from the side, lower Height until the circles lie flat.
  5. Move frames, then drag the small "You" box onto your feet if the circles sit off center. Lock frames when done.

Zooming later makes flat circles wrong until you change Size per yard again.

Limits

  • Trees still draw over the world. The client gives addons no access to the 3D scene; the ring and circles are drawn above it instead.
  • Name plates jump apart in crowds. The client places them with a solver of its own and an addon only sees where it put them. Hiding plain enemies (/slite mobs hide) keeps crowds calm.
  • Ground circles assume flat ground and a camera that orbits a point 1.5 yd above your feet.
  • Minimap pins assume 1.12's minimap scale and that the run's lair and chest coordinates are those of your zone's map.
  • Skill circles need the skill's tooltip to name its reach in yards. A buff or a self heal is not offered.
  • The leash is learned from the run's sleep state, so a guardian that falls asleep for another reason teaches a wrong value. The slider replaces it.

Problems

An error window in Alterac Valley

"QuestTimerFrame:SetPoint(): Couldn't find region named 'MinimapCluster'" comes from the game's own layout code, which cannot find the minimap frame by name there. Since 1.7.1 SurvivorsLite catches only that error and still places the chat dock and bags.

A red "SurvivorsLite error" line in chat

The addon caught one of its own errors and kept running. Please open a bug report with the line and a screenshot.

A skill is missing from the skill circles

Its tooltip names its reach in a way the addon does not read yet. A screenshot of the tooltip in a bug report lets it be added.

Feature status

Every feature passes the test harness, which runs the addon against the game's own addon API in a strict mock of the 1.12 client. Not every one has been seen in the real game yet. This page says which.

Tried one of the unconfirmed ones? Tell how it went with a feature check. A screenshot helps most.

Status as of version 1.7.1 (2026-10-29).

seen in game Confirmed by player screenshots and reports

  • Hero barLevel, health and the experience line under your character (1.0, smaller since 1.2).
  • Buffs, debuffs and buff uptimeIcons with time left and the uptime percent under timed buffs (1.2).
  • Class colored ringMarks where you stand under tree tops (1.0).
  • Boss and guardian platesBars with health for the final boss, bosses and guardians (1.0).
  • Thin bars for plain enemiesShown since 1.2. They still jump apart in crowds, see below.
  • Boss status listOne line per boss and guardian (1.0, one line each since 1.2).
  • Range and skill circles, flatThe range circle (1.1) and skill circles at each skill's reach (1.3), drawn flat on the screen.

not confirmed Built and tested, waiting for a check in game

  • Reset range (leash) of the guardiansThe leash bar over you while a guardian chases you, learning the real distance from play, leash rings on the minimap and the "20/40 yd" lines in the status list (1.2, 1.5). Distances need your map position, so they do not show without a zone map.
  • Circles on the groundReads the camera through saved view 5 (1.4). Whether this client writes live values there is unknown; if not, the circles stay flat and /slite camera says "Waiting for the camera".
  • Exact skill yards per skillSince 1.4 each skill circle uses the yards in its own tooltip, so Area only grows the skills it applies to.
  • Minimap pins for lairs and chestsThey assume 1.12's minimap scale (1.2).
  • Guardian marks and the next respawnOne mark per lair on its pin, line and plate; the lair that comes back first glows and counts down (1.7).
  • Shield on the health barFrom the shield the run reports next to your health (1.6).
  • Floating action barWith cooldown seconds and the talents' clocks (1.2).
  • Gear buttonsOn the minimap and the action bar to open the options (1.2).
  • Debuffs and absolute health on boss barsYour debuffs with stacks and time left under the bar (1.2).
  • Matching same-named units by healthSo an elite pack mob named like a guardian is not marked (1.2).
  • Party frames and party platesHealth, level, damage per second and revive progress; class colored plates with an arrow (1.0).
  • WarningsBoss countdown, "Name is down!" and the low health screen edge (1.0).
  • Alterac Valley error fixCatches the game's "Couldn't find region named 'MinimapCluster'" error (1.7.1).

not possible Tried and out of an addon's reach

  • See-through treesThe client gives addons no access to the 3D scene. The ring and circles are drawn above the world instead.
  • Overlapping name platesThe client places plates with a solver of its own and ignores their size; shrinking them (1.2) did not help and was removed in 1.4. Hiding plain enemies (/slite mobs hide) keeps crowds calm.
  • Bosses on the minimapThe game sends positions for lairs and chests but not for bosses.
